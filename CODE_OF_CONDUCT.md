# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][cc] as its code of
conduct. The full text is available at the link below, and we expect everyone
who participates, in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project
space, to follow it.

In short: be respectful and professional. We want localmem to be a welcoming,
harassment-free space for contributors of every background and experience level.
Disrespectful, abusive, or harassing behavior is not tolerated.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the
project maintainer at vjyadav193@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially.
The maintainer will review and respond as promptly as is reasonable, and may
take any action deemed appropriate, up to and including a ban from project
spaces.
